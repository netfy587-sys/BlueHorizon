Worth a Look Today: 8 Beach Destinations

Eight popular coasts, each with its own mood and best season. See what everyone loves, then Discover Your Next Pick.

South Asia

Goa, India

Long sandy beaches, lively beach shacks, old churches and relaxed evenings. North Goa is livelier; South Goa is quieter.

Best time: November to February · Great for: relaxing, food, nightlife

Indian Ocean

Maldives

Clear lagoons, coral reefs and resorts on tiny islands. Snorkelling and diving are the stars.

Best time: December to April · Great for: couples, snorkelling, luxury

Bay of Bengal

Andaman Islands, India

White beaches, mangroves and some of India’s best diving. Many islands are protected, so rules and permits apply.

Best time: November to April · Great for: diving, quiet beaches

Southeast Asia

Bali, Indonesia

Surf breaks, rice terraces, temples and cafés in one island. Different coasts have very different moods.

Best time: April to October · Great for: culture, surfing, wellness

Mediterranean

Santorini, Greece

Whitewashed villages above a dramatic volcanic bay, with black and red sand beaches below.

Best time: May to October · Great for: views, sunsets, couples

Andaman Sea

Phuket, Thailand

A big, varied island with lively resorts and quiet bays. Boats run to nearby islands for snorkelling.

Best time: November to April · Great for: families, island hopping

East Africa

Zanzibar, Tanzania

Powder-white sand, turquoise water, spice farms and the old Stone Town. Tides change the beach dramatically.

Best time: June to October · Great for: culture, beaches

Pacific coast

Gold Coast, Australia

A long strip of surf beaches, theme parks and hinterland rainforest, all close together.

Best time: September to November and March to May · Great for: families, surfing

Make A Choice Today: What Kind of Trip Do You Want?

A great holiday is a good match between you and the place. Start with the experience you want most.

Relax and recharge

Pick a calm beach with shade, good food nearby and easy transport. Resorts with kids’ clubs or spas make the trip effortless.

Adventure and water sports

Look for coasts with steady waves or reefs: surfing, snorkelling, diving, kayaking or paddleboarding. Check that operators are licensed.

Family-friendly

Choose shallow, gentle water, lifeguards, shaded areas and short transfers. Always keep children within arm’s reach near water.

Culture and food

Pair beaches with towns, markets and temples. Staying near a town makes it easier to explore without long drives.

Budget-smart

Travel just outside peak season, stay slightly away from the main beach strip and eat where locals eat.

Honeymoon and couples

Look for private beaches, sunset viewpoints and quiet bays. Book popular places well in advance.

A New Choice Awaits: Plan Your Trip in 6 Steps

Planning does not have to be stressful. Follow this order and every decision gets easier.

Offbeat idea

Try a smaller island or a quieter stretch of coast one hour from the famous beach. You often get calmer water, lower prices and a more local feel.

  1. Pick your dates

    Choose travel dates in the dry or shoulder season for good weather and fewer crowds.

  2. Set a rough budget

    List flights, stay, food, transport and activities. Add a buffer of about 10 to 15 percent for surprises.

  3. Book the hard parts first

    Flights and stays fill up first, especially near holidays. Read recent reviews and cancellation rules.

  4. Check entry and health rules

    Passports usually need months of validity, and some countries need visas or health documents. Check official government sites.

  5. Plan two or three highlights

    A boat trip, a sunset spot, a local meal. Leave other days free for rest.

  6. Sort insurance and backups

    Travel insurance, copies of documents and offline maps save a lot of stress.

Packing Checklist

Pack light, but do not skip the essentials.

Sun and skin

Broad-spectrum sunscreen SPF 30 or higher, a hat, sunglasses, light long sleeves and aloe or after-sun lotion.

Beach gear

Quick-dry towel, swimwear (two sets), flip-flops, a dry bag for phones and a reusable water bottle.

Smart extras

Power bank, universal adapter, waterproof phone pouch, small first-aid kit and any personal medicines.

Documents

Passport, tickets, hotel confirmations and insurance copies on paper and on your phone.

Beach Safety and Responsible Travel

The best holidays are safe ones. These habits keep you, other travellers and the coast healthy.

Before you get in the water

  • Swim only where lifeguards are present, and read the local warning flags.
  • Spot a rip current: a calm-looking channel of darker, choppy water moving out to sea.
  • If caught, float, stay calm and swim parallel to the shore, then angle back.
  • Drink water regularly; heat and sun can hit harder than you expect.
  • Never touch or stand on coral and keep a respectful distance from wildlife.
  • Take your rubbish with you and use reef-friendly sunscreen where you can.

Frequently asked questions

What is BlueHorizon?

BlueHorizon is a beach holiday planning guide. It helps you choose a destination, pick the best season, pack well and stay safe by the sea.

When is the best time to visit a beach destination?

It depends on the region. Many tropical beaches are best in the dry season, which usually means calmer seas, more sunshine and fewer storms. Check the local climate before you book.

How can I stay safe in the water?

Swim at beaches with lifeguards, read warning flags, never swim alone or after drinking alcohol, and learn how to spot a rip current.

What should I do if I am caught in a rip current?

Stay calm, float, and swim parallel to the shore until you are out of the current. Then swim back at an angle. Wave for help if you cannot get free.

What sunscreen should I use at the beach?

Choose a broad-spectrum sunscreen with SPF 30 or higher, apply it generously and reapply every two hours and after swimming. Reef-friendly options are kinder to coral.

How can I travel responsibly?

Take your rubbish away, never touch or stand on coral, keep distance from wildlife and support local businesses.
